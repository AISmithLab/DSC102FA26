## Overview

In this challenge, you will set up a local AI-assisted coding environment and use AI agents to solve a real software bug-fix problem from AI Coding Gym. Your goal is to produce a correct, well-tested fix while learning how to inspect, direct, and improve an AI agent's work.

Frontier AI agents often get close without fully resolving a bug. Careful diagnosis, targeted prompting, code inspection, and regression testing can make the difference.

## Learning Loop

1. **Inspect the AI agent's work.** Download the agent's code and reasoning trace from the platform. Study how it diagnosed the bug, which files it changed, what tests it ran, and what it overlooked.
2. **Attempt the challenge using AI as a tool.** Work in your local coding environment with AI coding assistants. Use your own judgment to redirect the agent, revise its changes, and strengthen its tests. You may use any AI tools you like.
3. **Submit and compare results.** Submit your solution through AI Coding Gym and compare its result with the AI baseline and your peers.
4. **Share work in progress in class.** We will dedicate class time for two or three students to present their approaches for 10 minutes each. Presentations will be selected for methodological diversity, not only successful outcomes. Share what you tried, what worked, and what did not.
5. **Write a reflection.** Submit the structured reflection described below.

## Deliverables

### 1. Challenge Submission via AI Coding Gym

Submit your bug fix on AI Coding Gym. You may submit as many times as the platform permits; your best result will be recorded.

### 2. AI Interaction History via Canvas

Submit the complete interaction history from the AI tools you used, including your prompts, the AI's responses, and meaningful iterations leading to the final solution. Export instructions will be provided with the setup details.

### 3. Final Report via Canvas

Submit a three-to-five-paragraph PDF report containing:

- **AI inspection summary:** What did the AI agent do? What were its key diagnostic and implementation decisions? What weaknesses did you identify in its reasoning trace?
- **Your strategy:** What did you change and why? Describe your approach, the prompts you used, and the software-engineering judgment you applied that the AI did not.
- **Results comparison:** Compare your result with the AI baseline. Include a screenshot of your AI Coding Gym result or leaderboard position.
- **Reflection:** Answer all three questions:
  - Where did the AI fall short?
  - What did I do that the AI did not?
  - What concept or skill did this teach me?

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Challenge result on AI Coding Gym | 30% |
| Reflection write-up, evaluated for depth of analysis rather than only the result | 40% |
| AI diagnosis, including identification of errors and weaknesses in the AI's approach | 30% |

A student who does not outperform the AI baseline but writes a perceptive reflection that correctly identifies why and what concept to strengthen can still earn a strong grade. Understanding matters more than the raw result.
