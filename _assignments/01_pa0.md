## Overview

Select one problem from the [Human-SWE-Bench Editor's Picks list on AI Coding Gym](https://aicodinggym.com/challenges/?view=picks&type=Human-SWE-Bench) to complete for this assignment.

In this challenge, you will set up a local AI-assisted coding environment and use AI agents to solve a real software bug-fix problem from AI Coding Gym. Your goal is to produce a correct, well-tested fix while learning how to inspect, direct, and improve an AI agent's work.

Frontier AI agents often get close without fully resolving a bug. Careful diagnosis, targeted prompting, code inspection, and regression testing can make the difference.

## Learning Loop

1. **Inspect the AI agent's work.** Download the agent's code and reasoning trace from the platform. Study how it diagnosed the bug, which files it changed, what tests it ran, and what it overlooked.
2. **Attempt the challenge using AI as a tool.** Work in your local coding environment with AI coding assistants. Use your own judgment to redirect the agent, revise its changes, and strengthen its tests. You may use any AI tools you like.
3. **Submit and compare results.** Submit your solution through AI Coding Gym and compare its result with the AI baseline and your peers.
4. **Verify your solution against the golden result.** Use the platform's comparison feature to compare your solution with the ground-truth solution. Review the differences and confirm why your fix is correct.
5. **Write a reflection.** Submit the structured reflection described below.

**Note:** A few groups may volunteer to share their experience during discussion for extra credit (see arrangements below). Describe the approach your group tried, where the AI struggled, how you evaluated or redirected it, and what you learned. Both successful and unsuccessful attempts are valuable because they help the class compare problem-solving strategies.
