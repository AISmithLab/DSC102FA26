## Overview

In this challenge, you will complete the course's existing AWS and Dask setup tasks through [AI Coding Gym](https://aicodinggym.com). You will use an AI coding assistant while retaining responsibility for verifying configuration choices, diagnosing failures, and documenting a reproducible environment.

## Learning Loop

1. **Inspect the AI agent's work.** Review the agent's code, commands, and reasoning trace. Identify its assumptions about your local environment, AWS configuration, dependencies, and Dask execution.
2. **Attempt the challenge using AI as a tool.** Complete the setup in your local coding environment. Use your own judgment to correct unsafe, incomplete, or environment-specific suggestions.
3. **Submit and compare results.** Submit the required artifacts on AI Coding Gym and compare your result with the AI baseline and your peers.
4. **Share work in progress in class.** Two or three students will present 10-minute accounts of their approaches. Selection is based on methodological diversity, not only top results.
5. **Write a reflection.** Submit the structured reflection described below.

## Deliverables

1. **Challenge submission via AI Coding Gym.** Submit the required setup artifacts. You may submit as many times as the platform permits; your best result will be recorded.
2. **AI interaction history via Canvas.** Submit the complete history of prompts, AI responses, and meaningful iterations that influenced your final work.
3. **Final report via Canvas.** Submit a three-to-five-paragraph PDF containing:
   - **AI inspection summary:** What did the agent do, what assumptions did it make, and what weaknesses did you find?
   - **Your strategy:** What did you change and why? Include important prompts and the systems judgment you applied.
   - **Results comparison:** Compare your result with the AI baseline and include a screenshot of your AI Coding Gym result or leaderboard position.
   - **Reflection:** Where did the AI fall short? What did you do that it did not? What concept or skill did this teach you?

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Challenge result on AI Coding Gym | 30% |
| Reflection write-up, evaluated for depth of analysis rather than only the result | 40% |
| AI diagnosis, including identification of errors and weaknesses in the AI's approach | 30% |

Not outperforming the AI baseline does not prevent a strong grade when the reflection perceptively explains why and identifies the concept that needs improvement. Understanding matters more than the raw result.
