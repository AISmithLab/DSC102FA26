## Overview

In this challenge, you will complete the course's existing data-exploration assignment with Dask through [AI Coding Gym](https://aicodinggym.com). Your goal is to produce a correct and scalable analysis while using AI as a collaborator and critically evaluating its choices.

## Learning Loop

1. **Inspect the AI agent's work.** Review the agent's code and reasoning trace. Study how it loaded, partitioned, cleaned, and explored the data, and note any correctness or scalability problems.
2. **Attempt the challenge using AI as a tool.** Work in your local coding environment with AI assistants. Apply your own judgment to improve the analysis and override weak decisions.
3. **Submit and compare results.** Submit the required artifacts on AI Coding Gym and compare your result with the AI baseline and your peers.
4. **Share work in progress in class.** Two or three students will present their approaches for 10 minutes each. Selection is based on methodological diversity, not only top results.
5. **Write a reflection.** Submit the structured reflection described below.

## Deliverables

1. **Challenge submission via AI Coding Gym.** Submit the required analysis artifacts. You may submit as many times as the platform permits; your best result will be recorded.
2. **AI interaction history via Canvas.** Submit the complete history of prompts, AI responses, and meaningful iterations that influenced your final work.
3. **Final report via Canvas.** Submit a three-to-five-paragraph PDF containing:
   - **AI inspection summary:** What did the agent do? What were its key data-processing decisions, and what weaknesses did you identify?
   - **Your strategy:** What did you change and why? Include important prompts and the domain or systems judgment you applied.
   - **Results comparison:** Compare your result with the AI baseline and include a screenshot of your AI Coding Gym result or leaderboard position.
   - **Reflection:** Where did the AI fall short? What did you do that it did not? What concept or skill did this teach you?

## Grading

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Challenge result on AI Coding Gym | 30% |
| Reflection write-up, evaluated for depth of analysis rather than only the result | 40% |
| AI diagnosis, including identification of errors and weaknesses in the AI's approach | 30% |

Not outperforming the AI baseline does not prevent a strong grade when the reflection perceptively explains why and identifies the concept that needs improvement. Understanding matters more than the raw result.
