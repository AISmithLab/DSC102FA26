## Use a local AI coding agent

Complete PA3 with a **local AI coding agent**, such as [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli) or [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs). A local agent runs on your computer, reads and edits your files, and runs terminal commands for you. Chat-based assistants such as the ChatGPT or Claude websites are **not** suitable: they cannot run your experiments and do not produce the session logs you must submit.

## AI interaction history

The AI interaction history is worth **3 points**, and the file format is part of the grade. Please upload the **complete, original** PA3 session logs to Canvas:

- Export your sessions with [CLIcodeLog](https://github.com/AISmithLab/clicodelog) using the **Raw** button, **not Export**.
- Submit a single `.json` or `.jsonl` file. If you have several sessions, put the original files in `session_log.zip`.
- Do not edit, summarize, or copy and paste the logs, and do not convert them to PDF, Markdown, or screenshots.
- You earn 1 point each for completeness, original logs, and correct format.

See the step-by-step [AI conversation history export instructions]({{ '/assignments/#export-ai-conversation-history' | prepend: site.baseurl }}).
