## Use a local AI coding agent

You must complete PA4 with a **local AI coding agent** that has terminal access and produces original session logs, such as [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli) or [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs).

## AI interaction history

Every team must upload the **complete, original** PA4 session logs to Canvas. This is worth **3 points**.

- Export your sessions with [CLIcodeLog](https://github.com/AISmithLab/clicodelog) using the **Raw** button.
- Submit the original `.json` or `.jsonl` export. If you have several sessions, put the original files in `session_log.zip`.
- Cite one exchange in your report and explain how you verified the assistant’s advice.

If you use AI to polish your report, attach the conversation history for that editing session.
